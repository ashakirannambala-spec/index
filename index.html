Who This Is For

The Parent

Thoughtful.
Forward-looking.

  • 38–52 years old, CXO / senior manager / entrepreneur
  • Progressive, well-educated, and financially stable
  • Senses their child is meant for something bigger
  • Active in premium professional and social networks
  • Open-minded — not marks-obsessed
The Child

Bright.
Curious. Searching.

  • 13–19 years old, with real sparks in sports, art, music, or leadership
  • Possibly force-fed academics, or high-performing but purposeless
  • Open to structured growth and honest reflection
  • Needs identity clarity, not just better grades
  • Ready to ask bigger questions about themselves
